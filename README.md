# EC & CCAC - Extracurricular & Co-Curricular Activities Committee

Official website portal for the Extracurricular & Co-Curricular Activities Committee (EC & CCAC) and SICO (Student Initiatives & Community Outreach) of R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering.

## Features
- **Hero Section**: Welcome banner with direct links to explore events and register for SICO via Google Forms.
- **About Us**: Mission and statistics counter (50+ Events, 1000+ Active Participants, 13+ Clubs), paired with a continuous auto-playing 15-image carousel with progress bar and shine animation.
- **Our Events**: Showcase of major campus fests and events (Cultural Fest, Club Waltz, Art & Design Expo, Music Festival, Literary Fest, Film Screening Night).
- **SICO Section**: Spotlight on Student Initiatives and Community Outreach programs.
- **Reports & Publications**: Directory previews for annual reports, event summaries, and event galleries.
- **Our Team**: Infinite continuous horizontal marquee carousel featuring Student Coordinators with smooth hover pauses.
- **Gallery**: Dynamic image gallery with responsive grid layout and interactive Lightbox modal.
- **Get In Touch & Footer**: Contact details, social links (Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram), address, and quick links.
