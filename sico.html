GET INVOLVED

Launch Your Own Initiative

Have an idea that could benefit students or the community? SICO provides the platform, funding, and mentorship to bring it to life.

📋

1. Draft Your Proposal

Prepare a 1-page overview detailing your project goals, target audience, timeline, and estimated resource requirements.

👥

2. Review with Coordinators

Meet with the EC & CCAC student coordinators to refine the concept, logistics, and institutional permissions.

🚀

3. Execute on Campus

Launch with full promotional support across college networks, auditorium spaces, and institutional backing.
